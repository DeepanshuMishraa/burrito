## Design Context

### Users
Privacy-conscious Mac users who record meetings, lectures, calls, and ideas and want useful notes without sending audio or transcripts to a cloud service.

### Brand Personality
Calm, exacting, and quietly confident. Burrito should feel native to the Mac, deeply private, and pleasant enough to become a daily tool.

### Aesthetic Direction
Refined minimalism with warm paper neutrals, near-black typography, and a restrained burnt-orange accent. Spacious editorial composition, sharp geometry, excellent typography, and authentic product imagery. Avoid generic SaaS gradients, glassmorphism, pill overload, decorative dashboards, and synthetic AI-looking illustrations.

### Design Principles

- Make privacy concrete through plain language, not security theatre.
- Let the real product carry the visual story.
- Use hierarchy, rhythm, and typography before decoration.
- Keep interactions quiet, fast, and respectful of reduced-motion preferences.
- Prefer a small number of precise elements over feature-section sprawl.
